About This Retro Game

Released in 1991, the game improves upon its predecessor with enhanced graphics, a more complex troop management system, and the introduction of Zhuge Liang 's 'Eight Diagrams Formation 'battle mechanics. Players control up to 5 characters simultaneously in grid-based combat.

The game spans key historical events from the Battle of Changban to the conquest of Nanman, blending history with fantasy elements like magical attacks and mythical creatures. Character progression includes both statistical growth and learnable tactics.

Praised for its deep strategic gameplay and faithful adaptation of Romance of the Three Kingdoms lore, it remains one of the most sophisticated RPGs on the NES platform, though its difficulty and Japanese-language dependence limited Western accessibility.
