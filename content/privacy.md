# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 4, 2026**

## Introduction

RetroVault ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website **retrovault.cc**.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information

When you access our site, certain information is automatically collected:

- **Browser type and version**
- **Device type and screen resolution**
- **Pages visited and time spent**
- **Referring website address**
- **IP address** (anonymized where possible)

This data is used for analytics and site improvement only.

### Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal cookies and browser local storage for:

| Purpose | Storage Type | Duration |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Theme preference (dark/light) | Local Storage | Persistent |
| Recently played games | Local Storage | Persistent |
| Analytics (page views) | Session Cookie | Session |

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

### Information You Voluntarily Provide

- **Username** — stored locally if you choose to set a nickname
- **Game progress/scores** — stored locally in your browser

We do **not** require account registration to use the service.

## How We Use Your Information

- Operate and maintain the website
- Improve user experience and game recommendations
- Analyze site traffic and usage patterns
- Serve contextual advertisements (see below)

## Third-Party Services

### Advertising

We display ads via **Adsterra**, **HilltopAds**, and **PropellerAds**. These ad networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads. Please refer to their respective privacy policies:

- [Adsterra Privacy Policy](https://adsterra.com/privacy/)
- [HilltopAds Privacy Policy](https://hilltopads.com/privacy.html)
- [PropellerAds Privacy Policy](https://propellerads.com/privacy/)

### Analytics

We use privacy-focused analytics to understand site usage. No personal data is shared with third-party analytics providers.

### EmulatorJS

Game emulation is powered by EmulatorJS (CDN). No game data or personal information is transmitted to the emulation service.

## Data Storage & Security

- **All game data** is served from Cloudflare's global CDN
- **ROM files** are stored on Cloudflare R2
- **No user data is stored on our servers**
- All data resides in your browser's local storage

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- **Access** personal data we hold about you
- **Delete** your data (clear your browser's local storage)
- **Opt out** of cookie-based advertising

To exercise these rights, contact us at **retro@vault.cc**.

## Children's Privacy

RetroVault is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

## Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach us at **retro@vault.cc**.
